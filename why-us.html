What makes us different?

Get the Right Diagnosis First

A transmission problem doesn't always mean you need a complete rebuild or replacement. Issues like faulty sensors, shift solenoids, electrical problems, fluid leaks, or other components can sometimes cause symptoms that feel like a major transmission failure.

That's why proper diagnosis comes first, and it's always free. We carefully inspect your vehicle, identify the source of the problem, and explain your repair options before moving forward. Our goal is to recommend what your vehicle actually needs, nothing more.

Over 30 Years of Transmission Experience

Transmission problems rarely get better on their own. Catching an issue early can help prevent additional damage and potentially save you from a much more expensive repair.
