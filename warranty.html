FAQs

Warranty FAQ

How long is the standard warranty?+

Qualifying Jack Wen transmission work comes with a standard 6-month / 25,000 km warranty.

Do you offer extended warranty coverage?+

Yes, extended coverage of up to 5 years / 250,000 km may be available for qualifying transmission work. Ask us for details specific to your vehicle and repair.

How do I know if my repair qualifies?+

Coverage can vary by vehicle and repair. The best way to know exactly what applies to you is to ask us directly, before or after your repair.

What should I do if I experience a problem after my repair?+

Contact Jack Wen and let us know what you're experiencing. We'll have you bring the vehicle in so we can inspect it and explain the next steps.

Can I ask about warranty coverage before approving the repair?+

Yes. You're welcome to ask about applicable warranty coverage before any work begins.
