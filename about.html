How We Started

Fix it right the first time

Jack Wen Transmission started with one mechanic, one bay, and one simple standard: fix it right the first time. More than 30 years later, that same standard still guides the way we work.

Every transmission that comes through our shop gets the attention we'd expect for our own vehicle. We've built our reputation largely through referrals, repeat customers, and quality workmanship because, to us, a transmission shop is only as good as the work it puts back on the road.

Today, Jack Wen has grown into a full-service transmission and drivetrain shop serving Scarborough, the GTA, and surrounding areas. From routine maintenance and advanced diagnostics to CVT service, complete rebuilds and performance applications, we have the experience and equipment to handle a wide range of transmission needs.

What We Do

  • Transmission Rebuilds

    When a transmission needs more than a basic repair, we take it apart, inspect the internal components, replace worn or damaged parts, and carefully rebuild the unit for dependable performance.

  • Transmission & CVT Repairs

    Slipping, jerking, delayed engagement, hard shifts, leaks or unusual noises? We diagnose the cause and repair what actually needs attention. Not every transmission problem requires a complete rebuild.

  • Transmission Diagnostics

    Transmission problems aren't always purely mechanical. We use professional diagnostic equipment combined with hands-on inspection to identify mechanical, electrical and computer-related issues before recommending repairs.

  • Transmission Service & Maintenance

    Proper maintenance can help extend transmission life and catch developing problems early. We provide fluid and filter services, inspections, leak checks and preventative maintenance to help keep your transmission performing properly.

  • Replacement Transmissions

    When replacing a transmission makes more sense than rebuilding it, we can source and install the appropriate replacement unit for your vehicle and walk you through the available options.

  • Differential & Drivetrain Service

    Your transmission is only one part of the system that puts power to the road. We also diagnose and service differentials and related drivetrain components to keep everything working together properly.

  • Performance Transmission Builds

    For vehicles that demand more than factory performance, we can build transmission solutions for high-output, towing and performance applications, with components selected around the demands of the vehicle.

Whatever you drive, daily driver, work truck, tow rig, or performance build, it starts with getting the right diagnosis and the right service.
